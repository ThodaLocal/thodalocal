Trust layer

Make trust visible.

The verification badge should be useful, not decorative. Customers should understand what it means before they order.

ThodaLocal Verified

A store gets the badge only after the verification process you define. Keep the standard clear and consistent as the platform grows.

✓Store identity and basic details reviewed
✓Hygiene / operational check where applicable
✓Verification status stays visible to customers

Local should feel personal.

ThodaLocal is not trying to hide small businesses behind a giant catalogue. The goal is to help neighbourhood stores get found, trusted and ordered from online.

How it works

Four simple steps.

The product is being built in stages. This is the intended customer journey once participating businesses are onboarded.

01

Discover

Search stores, categories and neighbourhoods in one place.

02

Verify

Look for the ThodaLocal verification status before you buy.

03

Order

Place an order through the supported flow once participating stores are available.

04

Receive

Receive your order through the delivery or fulfilment option offered by the participating store.
